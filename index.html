Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Editing happens locally in your browser. The AI background remover downloads its model to your device, but your image itself is not uploaded.

How do I compress an image without losing quality?

Export as WebP or JPEG and lower the quality slider a little. Around 75–85% usually looks the same while cutting the file size a lot. The editor shows the real saving.

Which formats can I export?

WebP, JPEG and PNG. You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and GIF. Use PNG or WebP to keep a transparent background.

Does the background remover work on real photos?

Yes. The AI mode handles people, products and animals. The plain-color mode is for white or solid backgrounds only.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no upload limit because nothing is uploaded. Very large images depend on your device memory.

Is PixelForge free?

Yes, every tool is free with no signup.
